Neuro-Surgical Specializations

Our multidisciplinary team leverages the latest technology to perform complex procedures safely.

Brain Surgery

Advanced treatment for aneurysms, tumors, vascular malformations, and functional disorders using neuro-navigation systems.

Spine Surgery

Comprehensive care for spinal stenosis, herniated discs, and spinal deformities, prioritizing minimally invasive techniques.

Neuro-Oncology

Targeted surgical interventions for brain and spinal cord tumors, working in tandem with oncologists for comprehensive care.

Pediatric Neurosurgery

Specialized, gentle care for congenital anomalies, hydrocephalus, and pediatric tumors in a child-friendly environment.

Hospital Information

Our History & Mission

Established in 2005, Rainbow Neuro Surgical Hospital was founded with a singular vision: to bring world-class neurological care to our community. Our mission is to restore neurological function and improve the quality of life for our patients through ethical, evidence-based practices.

Facilities & Infrastructure

Our 150-bed facility features 8 ultra-modern operation theaters equipped with intraoperative MRI, neuro-navigation systems, and highly advanced neuromonitoring setups. Our 24/7 Neuro-ICU maintains a 1:1 nurse-to-patient ratio.
